The reasarch have been conducted to compare the efficiency of studying with music on and studying with music off. The experiment will begin with multiple method to observe the impact on the retention of a person that are studying, therefore, multiple approach will be testing to get a result that will be use in efficiency calculation. The approach being:

1. studying with music with random genre of music
2. studying with music with specific genre that suits the mood of the listener
3. studying without music
4. studying with music but with lofi kind of music
5. studying without music in completely silent room
6. studying with music with emotional music
7. studying with music but with nature sound

The effectiveness of the studying will be determine by how long the listener can focus without getting distracted with his surrounding..
